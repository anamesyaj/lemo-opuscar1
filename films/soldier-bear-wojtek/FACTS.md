# Historical Fact Sheet: *The Bear That Enlisted in the Polish Army (Corporal Wojtek, 1942–1944)*

Every historical claim in `films/soldier-bear-wojtek` is verified against documented scholarship, museum records, and primary archival accounts below.

## 1. Adoption in Iran (1942)
- **Date & Location**: April–August **1942**, on the road between Pahlavi and Tehran / near **Hamadan, Iran** (Persia).
- **Circumstances**: Newly evacuated soldiers and civilian refugees of the Polish II Corps (Anders' Army) purchased an orphaned Syrian brown bear cub (*Ursus arctos syriacus*) from a young Iranian boy. In August 1942 the bear was donated to the **22nd Artillery Supply Company** (*22 Kompania Zaopatrywania Artylerii*) and named **Wojtek** (`[ˈvɔjtɛk]`, nickname for Wojciech, meaning *"joyful warrior"*).
- **Care & Habits**: Fed condensed milk from an empty bottle by caretaker **Piotr Prendys** (alongside Henryk Zacharewicz and Dymitr Szawlugo), Wojtek grew up alongside the soldiers, learning to salute, wrestle playfully, march on his hind legs, and enjoy beer as a reward.
- **Sources**:
  - Encyclopædia Britannica, *"Wojtek the Bear | Private, Rank, Edinburgh Zoo, War, & Facts"*: https://www.britannica.com/animal/Wojtek-the-Bear
  - The British Library, *"Wojtek, the soldier bear from the Polish Army"* (citing Polish Institute and Sikorski Museum archives): https://www.bl.uk/stories/blogs/posts/wojtek-the-soldier-bear-from-the-polish-army

## 2. Official Enlistment at Port To Board Allied Transport Ship (1944)
- **Circumstances**: To sail from Egypt (`Port Tewfik` / Alexandria) to Italy with the Polish II Corps aboard the Allied transport ship *MS Batory*, British military transport regulations prohibited pet and mascot animals from boarding.
- **Solution**: Polish officers officially drafted Wojtek into the Polish Army as a **Private** (*Szeregowy*) in the **22nd Artillery Supply Company**, issuing him his own military paybook, rank, and service number (`253`) so he could board legally as an enlisted soldier.
- **Sources**:
  - Hoover Institution Library & Archives, *"Wojtek, the Bear of Monte Cassino"*: https://www.hoover.org/news/wojtek-bear-monte-cassino
  - Wikipedia / Polish Army Museum archival summary: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wojtek

## 3. Battle of Monte Cassino, Promotion to Corporal, & Official Emblem (May 1944)
- **Action**: During the **Battle of Monte Cassino** (Italy, May 1944), adult Wojtek (weighing up to ~200–500 lb across his service years) imitated his comrades by carrying heavy crates of 25-pounder artillery shells from supply trucks to gun positions under fire without dropping a single crate.
- **Honors**: In recognition of his service, Wojtek was promoted to the rank of **Corporal** (*Kapral*), and the **22nd Artillery Supply Company** adopted an official unit emblem depicting a brown bear carrying an artillery shell (painted on all company vehicles and badges). After WWII, Wojtek retired to Edinburgh Zoo in Scotland (`1947–1963`), where statues in Edinburgh, Kraków, Sopot, and Cassino commemorate him today.
