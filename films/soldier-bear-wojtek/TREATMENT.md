## 1. Three Candidate Structures (and Choice)

1. **Candidate A — Postwar Edinburgh Zoo Flashback**: Open at Edinburgh Zoo in 1947 with Polish veterans visiting an older bear behind bars, then flash back to WWII Iran and Monte Cassino.
   - *Verdict*: Rejected — opening behind zoo bars dulls the hook; the audience wants to see a 500-pound bear in uniform inside an active army camp at frame 0.
2. **Candidate B — Map & Regiment Dossier**: Trace Anders' Army across a painted map from Persia to Iraq, Egypt, and Italy with clipboard stamps at each border.
   - *Verdict*: Rejected — turns a physical comedy-and-heroism story into a travelogue.
3. **Candidate C — Direct Physical Escalation from Camp Mascot → Enlisted Private → Monte Cassino Corporal (CHOSEN)**:
   - **Hook (`0.0–4.9 s`)**: Open immediately on a full-grown 500-pound Syrian brown bear wearing a Polish garrison cap standing at crisp salute beside soldier Piotr in front of a timber-and-brick barracks fascia (`"22ND SUPPLY CO. · HAMADAN 1942"`), pulling back to reveal the desert supply compound and canvas-top army truck.
   - **Camp Training (`4.9–10.8 s`)**: Show orphaned cub Wojtek growing up in the Persian camp—drinking from a bottle, learning to salute, playfully wrestling Piotr, and chugging an amber beer bottle while soldiers cheer.
   - **Port Regulation & Enlistment Turn (`10.8–18.4 s`)**: At the Alexandria dock (`"ALEXANDRIA PORT · EMBARKATION"`), a stern British port officer in a peaked cap points at a `"NO PETS ABOARD"` sign and blocks the warship gangplank (**Silence #1** on `"banned pets"`). Immediately, the Polish officer stamps `"PRIVATE WOJTEK · #253"` onto an official military paybook, hands it to Wojtek, and a green checkmark badge pops as Wojtek marches up the gangplank saluting!
   - **Monte Cassino Artillery Proof (`18.4–25.7 s`)**: Under dusk artillery fire below the ruined hilltop abbey of Monte Cassino, cannons roar; after a dramatic pause (**Silence #2**), Wojtek steps up, grabs a massive **`100 LB AMMO`** wooden crate between both paws, and marches past stunned soldiers to stack it by the howitzer.
   - **Payoff & Rolling Truck Emblem Finale (`25.7–32.0 s`)**: The commander pins red-and-white **Corporal** chevrons onto Wojtek's collar while a green checkmark pops; then a 3D olive-drab supply truck rolls across the foreground with the official **white circular emblem of Wojtek carrying an artillery shell** painted on its door while Corporal Wojtek rides in the cab saluting!

## 2. Logline & Emotional Arc

- **Logline**: When British port rules barred the Polish Army's pet brown bear from boarding a WWII troopship, the soldiers officially enlisted him as Private Wojtek—and at Monte Cassino he carried 100-pound artillery crates under fire to earn promotion to Corporal.
- **Setup (`0.0–10.8 s`)**: Polish soldiers in Iran adopt orphaned bear cub Wojtek and raise him like a fellow soldier who salutes, wrestles, and drinks beer.
- **Complication / Turn (`10.8–18.4 s`)**: British port officials ban pets from boarding the warship to Italy—so the unit officially drafts Wojtek as a Private with his own paybook and serial number.
- **Climax & Payoff (`18.4–32.0 s`)**: At Monte Cassino, Wojtek hauls heavy crates of 25-pounder shells single-handedly without dropping a single one, earning promotion to Corporal and immortalization on the company's official emblem.
