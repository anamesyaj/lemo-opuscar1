- **Style**: `~/lemo-my-styles/baboon-cartoon-standard/STYLE.md` (`cartoon-short-baboon-standard`, modeled on *The Baboon Who Ran a Train Station*).
- **Format**: `1080×1920` (vertical 9:16), `30 fps`, target duration `~32.0s` (`960 frames`), `-14.0 ± 1.0 LUFS`, `<= -1.0 dBTP`.
- **Mandatory Baboon Rules Enforced**:

All 3 location/date headers are physical 3D painted wood-and-gold fascia signboards mounted on the Roman architecture (`PAPAL CRYPT · ROME / EXHUMATION · 897 AD`, `LATERAN BASILICA / SYNOD COURT · 897 AD`, `TIBER RIVER EMBANKMENT / PAPAL DECREE · 897 AD`).

  3. **Dense Modular `tbox` 3D Worlds**:
     - **Set 1 (Papal Crypt, Rome, 897 AD)**: Recessed Roman travertine stone arches, timber roof beams, carved stone sarcophagus with sliding lid (`FORMOSUS · D. 896`), iron shovels, and flickering bronze braziers.
     - **Set 2 (Lateran Basilica Synod Court, 897 AD)**: Grand Roman basilica interior with marble columns, coffered timber beams, prosecutor's carved oak pulpit (`STEPHEN VI`), elevated gold-and-velvet papal throne, and an inscribed stone tablet (`CANON LAW · 897`).

  4. **Prop-Gripping Character Acting**:
     - **Pope Formosus (The Skeleton Defendant)**: Wide hollow eye sockets with expressive cartoon brow ridges and a hinged jaw that clacks slightly when the deacon speaks; wears gold papal mitre and crimson-gold vestments, holding a golden papal crosier and raising 3 blessing fingers (`❌` badge pops when guilty verdict is struck!).
     - **Pope Stephen VI (The Unhinged Prosecutor)**: Red-faced, furious eyes, pointing accusingly from the oak pulpit, slamming a papal gavel/scroll (`ACTA NULLA`).
     - **The Trembling Young Deacon (Defense Lawyer)**: Sweating, wide-eyed, hiding behind the throne while holding up a `"DEFENSE BRIEF"` scroll and puppeteering the skeleton's arm to answer.

## 2. Three Candidate Structures & Selection
1. **Option A — Chronological Political Biography**: Covers Formosus's life as Bishop of Porto, his diplomatic missions to Bulgaria, the Spoletan faction, his death, the trial, and the four successor popes. *Rejected: too dry and crowded with names for a 32-second viral short.*
2. **Option B — Courtroom Comedy Escalation (Chosen)**: Opens immediately on the absurd visual hook of a dressed-up skeleton sitting on a courtroom throne in 897 AD (`0.0–3.8s`), flashes back to Pope Stephen VI ordering the grave dug up (`3.8–8.5s`), escalates through the terrified deacon appointed to speak for the silent skeleton (`8.5–18.5s`), lands the guilty verdict + 3-finger chop (`18.5–25.0s`), and finishes on the Tiber River aftermath and the church's permanent ban on putting dead people on trial (`25.0–32.0s`). *Selected: maximum visual comedy, tight physical prop acting, and an unforgettable historical punchline.*
3. **Option C — Forensic Reconstruction**: Treats the event as a dark crime investigation. *Rejected: doesn't fit the warm, punchy 2D+3D Baboon cartoon style.*

## 3. Spoken Script & 8-Shot Breakdown (~106 words, ~32.0s)
1. **Line 0 (Hook — Synod Court)**: `"In 897 AD, Pope Stephen the Sixth put his dead predecessor on trial for real."` (`0.0s–4.8s`)
   - *Visual*: Lateran Basilica (`LATERAN BASILICA / SYNOD COURT · 897 AD` fascia sign). Skeleton Pope Formosus sits slumped in full gold papal robes and mitre on a velvet throne while Pope Stephen VI points furiously from the oak pulpit.
2. **Line 1 (Exhumation — Papal Crypt)**: `"Nine months after Pope Formosus died, guards dug up his corpse and dressed the skeleton in gold robes."` (`4.8s–11.2s`)
   - *Visual*: Cut to the Papal Crypt (`PAPAL CRYPT · ROME / EXHUMATION · 897 AD` sign). Two papal guards with shovels slide open the stone sarcophagus (`FORMOSUS`) and place the gold mitre and crosier onto the bewildered skeleton.
3. **Line 2 (The Deacon Defense — Synod Court)**: `"They propped him on a throne and appointed a trembling young deacon to answer as his defense lawyer."` (`11.2s–17.2s`)
   - *Visual*: Back in the Lateran Basilica. The sweating young deacon peeks out from behind the throne holding a `"DEFENSE"` scroll and nervously waves the skeleton's bony hand while Stephen VI screams questions.
4. **Line 3 (The Guilty Verdict & 3 Fingers — Synod Court)**: `"The silent skeleton still lost the case, had his three blessing fingers chopped off, and was stripped of his title."` (`17.2s–24.2s`)
   - *Visual*: Red `❌` badge pops above the throne! Stephen VI slams his gavel on the pulpit (`GUILTY`), the skeleton's gold mitre pops off into a rough sackcloth tunic, and his raised right hand switches from 3 bony fingers to a bandaged stump with a comical cartoon `POP!`.
5. **Line 4 (The Twist Finale — Tiber Embankment)**: `"After his body was tossed into the Tiber River, Rome rebelled and banned putting dead people on trial forever."` (`24.2s–32.0s`)
   - *Visual*: Cut to the Tiber River Embankment (`TIBER RIVER EMBANKMENT / PAPAL DECREE · 897 AD` sign). Green `✅` badge pops above the carved stone monument (`NO DEAD ON TRIAL`) while a ceremonial papal barge glides past in the foreground carrying Formosus's restored mitre and crowds cheer.
